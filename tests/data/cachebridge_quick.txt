This document describes a small library inventory. Books arrive at the central desk each morning and are sorted by their shelf labels. The records contain a title, a catalog number, an author, and the date of receipt. Staff check the records before placing books on the shelves. Visitors can request a book using its catalog number. A daily report lists new arrivals, returned items, and requests that remain open. Each evening the staff compare the report against the shelf inventory and preserve a copy for the next shift.

This document describes a weather observation station. The station measures temperature, humidity, rainfall, and wind direction at fixed intervals throughout the day. The records include the observation time and the identity of the instrument. Technicians inspect the instruments each week and note any maintenance in a separate log. The daily summary contains the highest temperature, the lowest temperature, and the total rainfall. Researchers compare summaries from different days to study changes in local conditions. Missing observations are recorded explicitly rather than replaced by invented measurements.
